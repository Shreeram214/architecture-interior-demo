05 Our Process

From First Conversation to Final Detail.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    Understand the client's vision, lifestyle, requirements, site conditions and budget.

  2. Step 02

    Planning

    Develop layouts, space planning, zoning, circulation and initial concepts.

  3. Step 03

    Concept Design

    Explore design directions, mood boards, materials, colors and architectural ideas.

  4. Step 04

    Design Development

    Refine layouts, elevations, interiors, furniture, lighting and specifications.

  5. Step 05

    Execution

    Coordinate execution, materials, contractors, site visits and design details.

  6. Step 06

    Handover

    Review quality, complete finishing details and deliver the final space.
